Privacy Policy

Your project stays with you.

CinemaFrame and CinemaFrame DIR · Effective November 13, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No tracking, ads or analytics. The apps contain no third-party SDKs and don’t follow you across apps or websites.
  • We don’t collect your data. Shots, plans, rental orders and floor plans are stored on your device. We have no server that receives them.
  • Camera to director, directly. The live picture and takes travel between your own devices over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, not through the internet.
  • You decide what leaves the phone. Exports, backups and the diagnostic log go only where you send them.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the iOS apps CinemaFrame (the camera app for the camera team) and CinemaFrame DIR (the director’s monitor for iPhone and iPad), together “the apps”, handle information. “We” means the developer of the apps.

Information we collect

None. The apps don’t send personal information, usage statistics, crash reports or device identifiers to us or to anyone acting for us. The apps’ privacy manifests declare no collected data and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

To do their job, the apps keep the following on your iPhone or iPad, inside the apps’ own storage:

  • photos and videos you capture, with the details of each shot: camera, format, lens, focal length, look, scene and shot number, date and time;
  • if you turn on location for shots, where each shot was framed and which way the camera faced;
  • shooting plans, imported storyboards, rental orders and floor plans;
  • your settings, such as the chosen camera, lenses and viewfinder tools;
  • a diagnostic log of problems on set, such as camera interruptions, failed saves and dropped transfers.

This data is included in your device’s iCloud or computer backup if you have those backups turned on, under Apple’s terms. Deleting an app deletes its data from the device.

When information leaves your device

Between your own devices

When you pair CinemaFrame DIR with a camera phone by scanning its QR code, the live picture, takes, stars and floor plans are sent directly between the two devices over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. The QR code carries a pairing key, so only the device that scanned it can connect. You can remove paired directors in CinemaFrame’s settings.

The browser stream

If you turn on the browser stream, CinemaFrame shows the live picture to web browsers on the same network. The link contains an access token; anyone you give the link to can watch while the stream is on. Turn the stream off to end access.

Apple services

The sun and moon map, place search and place names use Apple Maps. When you use them, your search text or location is sent to Apple to return results, under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We don’t receive this information.

Storyboards from Google links

If you import a storyboard by pasting a Google Docs, Slides or Sheets link, the app downloads that document from Google as a PDF. Google receives the request as it would from any browser, under Google’s Privacy Policy. Nothing is sent to Google unless you paste such a link.

Things you choose to share

Exported PDFs, presentations, spreadsheets, backups, shared shots and the diagnostic log leave the device only when you share or save them, and go only where you send them. A PDF exported with full details includes the location and compass heading of each shot; the app tells you before you export it.

Children

The apps are tools for film crews and are not directed at children. Because we collect no personal information, we hold none about children.

Your choices

  • Turn camera, microphone, location, Photos, local network or Bluetooth access on or off in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off location for shots, or choose which details appear on the data strip under photos, in CinemaFrame’s settings.
  • Delete shots, plans, rental orders and floor plans in the apps, or delete an app to remove all of its data.

Changes to this policy

If the way the apps handle information changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the support contact listed on the apps’ App Store pages.
